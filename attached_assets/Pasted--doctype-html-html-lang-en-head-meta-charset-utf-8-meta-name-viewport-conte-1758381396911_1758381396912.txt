      <section id="owner-builder" aria-labelledby="owner-builder-h2">
        <h2 id="owner-builder-h2" style="padding:16px 12px 0; margin:0">Owner‑Builder Permitting</h2>
        <details>
          <summary><span class="q"><span class="dot"></span>Can I legally act as my own contractor to build a pool?</span></summary>
          <div class="a">Yes. In Florida, Statute 489.103(7) allows homeowners to act as their own contractor for pools built for personal use, provided you supervise the work and hire licensed subcontractors for regulated trades.</div>
        </details>
        <details>
          <summary><span class="q"><span class="dot"></span>Do I need to file an Owner‑Builder affidavit?</span></summary>
          <div class="a">Most counties require an affidavit acknowledging responsibility as the contractor, plus any county‑specific disclosures.</div>
        </details>
        <details>
          <summary><span class="q"><span class="dot"></span>How long does county plan review take?</span></summary>
          <div class="a">Typical reviews take 5–15 business days. Rechecks on corrections are commonly 2–5 business days depending on workload.</div>
        </details>
      </section>

      <section id="engineering" aria-labelledby="engineering-h2">
        <h2 id="engineering-h2" style="padding:16px 12px 0; margin:0">Engineering & Plans</h2>
        <details>
          <summary><span class="q"><span class="dot"></span>Do I need signed/sealed engineering drawings?</span></summary>
          <div class="a">Yes—most jurisdictions require sealed structural sheets, sections, and schedules, plus hydraulics/TDH and barrier details.</div>
        </details>
        <details>
          <summary><span class="q"><span class="dot"></span>What is a TDH worksheet and why is it required?</span></summary>
          <div class="a">Total Dynamic Head calculations show the hydraulic load so the pump, filter, and heater sizing meet code and efficiency requirements.</div>
        </details>
        <details>
          <summary><span class="q"><span class="dot"></span>What supporting documents are often missed?</span></summary>
          <div class="a">Commonly missed: VGB suction outlet compliance, energy efficiency form, barrier/fence details, and a correctly scaled plot plan.</div>
        </details>
      </section>

      <section id="costs" aria-labelledby="costs-h2">
        <h2 id="costs-h2" style="padding:16px 12px 0; margin:0">Costs & Timelines</h2>
        <details>
          <summary><span class="q"><span class="dot"></span>How much does a permitting package cost?</span></summary>
          <div class="a">Our typical permit coordination package is <strong>$895</strong>, which includes engineered drawings, TDH, barrier details, and submittal coordination.</div>
        </details>
        <details>
          <summary><span class="q"><span class="dot"></span>What other fees should I expect?</span></summary>
          <div class="a">Counties charge plan review and inspection fees, plus trade fees for electrical, gas, or screen enclosures where applicable.</div>
        </details>
        <details>
          <summary><span class="q"><span class="dot"></span>How long from submission to permit approval?</span></summary>
          <div class="a">On average, 2–4 weeks depending on county workload, completeness, and number of correction cycles.</div>
        </details>
      </section>

      <section id="diy-vs-builder" aria-labelledby="diy-vs-builder-h2">
        <h2 id="diy-vs-builder-h2" style="padding:16px 12px 0; margin:0">DIY vs Hiring</h2>
        <details>
          <summary><span class="q"><span class="dot"></span>What’s the difference between hiring a pool builder and acting as Owner‑Builder?</span></summary>
          <div class="a">Hiring a builder means they serve as contractor and assume liability. Owner‑Builder saves money but puts permit compliance and scheduling on you.</div>
        </details>
        <details>
          <summary><span class="q"><span class="dot"></span>Can you just prepare drawings and I handle the rest?</span></summary>
          <div class="a">Yes. We can provide engineered drawings only, or add permit coordination if you want us to manage submittals and resubmittals.</div>
        </details>
        <details>
          <summary><span class="q"><span class="dot"></span>Do you coordinate resubmittals if the county rejects a sheet?</span></summary>
          <div class="a">Yes—we handle plan reviewer comments, clouded revisions, and resubmittals until approval.</div>
        </details>
      </section>

      <section id="technical" aria-labelledby="technical-h2">
        <h2 id="technical-h2" style="padding:16px 12px 0; margin:0">Technical & Portals</h2>
        <details>
          <summary><span class="q"><span class="dot"></span>What if my PDF file is over 10MB?</span></summary>
          <div class="a">Many counties reject oversized files. We compress PDFs, split large sets, and export correctly so each PDF passes upload checks.</div>
        </details>
        <details>
          <summary><span class="q"><span class="dot"></span>How do I submit via Accela or Fast Track?</span></summary>
          <div class="a">We guide you step‑by‑step: account creation, selecting the correct permit type, uploading files, and clicking <em>Submission Complete</em> to start pre‑verification.</div>
        </details>
        <details>
          <summary><span class="q"><span class="dot"></span>Do I need a fence or alarm around my pool?</span></summary>
          <div class="a">Most jurisdictions require at least one barrier such as a fence, door alarms, or safety covers. Requirements vary by county; we include barrier details in every plan set.</div>
        </details>
      </section>
